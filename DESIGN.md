## Overview
Mawi's site is light, white-ground, with full-bleed photographic hero bands carrying white headlines and a single orange call to action. The orange is used sparingly for links and buttons.

## Colors
Ink #212529 on white for reading. Orange #F25A2C for actions and links only. Forest #0C2919 for dark bands. Peach #FDE2D9 and blush #FFF7F5 as soft section fills. Derived dark variant: forest-black ground (#0E1612), raised surface #17231C, ink #ECE8E4, orange lifted to #FF7A4D for contrast.

## Typography
Manrope for everything readable (Google Fonts). Roboto Condensed, widely tracked, for section labels (Google Fonts). Both are open fonts; no proprietary font files are used.

## Layout
Single column, content max ~1100px, generous vertical rhythm, wide gutters. Dark title band at top.

## Shapes and depth
Pill buttons (41px radius). Flat cards with hairline borders; shadow only on the one result card.

## Components
Pill buttons, chips for presets, status pills, comparison table, callouts.
